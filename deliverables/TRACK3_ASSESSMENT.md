Internal planning note, assessed against the published track text. Ratings are our judgement, not jury scores.

| Requirement | Status |
|---|---|

## Rubric
| Criterion | Rating | Evidence | Main gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Healthcare problem | Moderate | Published MSC work describes the need to expand products without inducing senescence | No statement yet from a lab on how it decides today or what failure costs |
| Innovation and technology | Moderate to strong | Tool refuses invalid data and caught a real labelling error; data stays in the browser; thresholds set by measurement; cross-cohort clock | Core score is published work; a single gene matches it on direction |
| Evidence | Strong for this stage | Two preregistered analyses, 19/22 donors in new cohorts, held-out clock, 39 automated tests, negative results reported | Retrospective; no per-sample assay; no prospective use |
| Feasibility | Strong technically | Runs in a browser with no install or server | RNA-seq cost and turnaround not measured |
| Implementation | Weak | Report format and pilot design exist | No pilot site, no validated threshold, no regulatory plan |
| Communication | Open | 7-minute script and Q&A drafted | Needs rehearsal and someone ready for biology questions |

## Conclusion
The project qualifies and fits the track. Evidence and the demo are now its strengths. Implementation remains the weakest criterion and the one most likely to decide the result.

## Priorities before the event
1. **Find one MSC lab as a pilot partner.** Even a letter of interest or a recorded conversation addresses the two weakest rows. Only the team can do this.
2. **Rehearse the demo** on the venue network, with the fallback ready.
3. **Measure one real workflow:** time and cost from sample to report in a partner lab.
4. **Confirm the abstract format** and the team list.

## Claims to avoid
"First", "release test", "predicts potency", "reduces cost", any acceptance threshold, and any partner, cost or regulatory statement that isn't documented. Odds of winning can't be estimated: the other entries and the rubric weights are unknown.
